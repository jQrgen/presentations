  2. PAPERConference paper, peer reviewed

    The Minimum Hybrid Contract (MHC): Combining Legal and Blockchain Smart Contracts

    EASE 2020, 24th International Conference on Evaluation and Assessment in Software Engineering, ACM June 2020

    Jørgen Svennevik Notland, Jakob Svennevik Notland, Donn Morrison

    Proposes the Minimum Hybrid Contract: an online smart contract immutably linked to an offline legal contract, so that a contract's financial transactions become auditable, transparent and immutable while disputes stay with the civil courts. Preprint on arXiv, February 2020.

    10 citations Google Scholar: 10 · ResearchGate: 6 · Semantic Scholar: 5 · Crossref: 4

    The Minimum Hybrid Contract (MHC): Combining Legal and Blockchain Smart Contracts

    NTNU School of Entrepreneurship, Norwegian University of Science and Technology June 2019

    Jørgen Svennevik Notland. Supervisors: Roger Sørheim and Donn Morrison

    Having identified contracts as the nexus of corruption, the thesis asks how contracts, blockchain smart contracts and financial regulation can be combined to make contracts secure, accountable, transparent and inclusive, and introduces the term Minimum Hybrid Contract.

  9. PAPERProject thesis

    Blockchain enabled Trust & Transparency in supply chains

    NTNU School of Entrepreneurship, Norwegian University of Science and Technology Autumn 2016, published February 2017

    Jørgen Svennevik Notland, Anders Viet Hua

    Investigates the nature of trust in supply chains and whether blockchain can increase it. Blockchain gives high integrity but unstable information reliability: how data is recorded on-chain is the critical step, and actors need a clear incentive before they adopt it.

    29 citations ResearchGate: 29 · Google Scholar: 17
